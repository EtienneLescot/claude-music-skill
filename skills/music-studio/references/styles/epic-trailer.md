# Epic trailer

Sounds like: Two Steps From Hell, Audiomachine, Hans Zimmer's blockbuster scores. For trailers, sports, game launches, "the stakes are high".

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo, metre | 4/4, 80 to 100 BPM often felt in half time; or 120 to 140 for a driving last act |
| Key, mode | minor (Aeolian), Dorian for hope inside the dark; a final turn to the relative major for triumph |
| Harmony | `i bVI bIII bVII`, `i bVI bVII i`, `i iv bVI V`; one chord per bar or per two bars |
| Melody | a short motif of 3 to 5 notes, big intervals (fifths, octaves), hammered in augmentation at the end |
| Rhythm | spiccato ostinato in 8ths or 16ths with `3+3+2` accents; big drums on 1 and the "and" of 2 |
| Orchestra | low strings and low brass carry the weight; horns and trumpets on the motif in octaves; choir on held chords; timpani, big drum, bass drum, cymbals |
| Form | three acts: mystery (sparse, a fragment of the motif), build (ostinato, rising register and density), climax (full tutti, motif in octaves); a stop or a single hit between acts |
| Signature devices | the braam (a long low brass chord, fortissimo, swelling), risers (cymbal swell, timpani roll), hits and silences, a final "button" (one short tutti chord) |
| Pitfalls | wall of sound from the first bar; no melody, only texture; climax with no new element; a fade-out instead of a button |
| In this studio | `trombones`, `bass trombones`, `tuba` long for braams; `violins 2`, `violas`, `celli` short for the ostinato; `horns`, `trumpets`, `violins` long on the motif; `choir` all sections; `big drum`, `timpani`, `cymbals` swell |
