# Wonder, magic

Sounds like: John Williams's fantasy scores, James Newton Howard, Alexandre Desplat, Danny Elfman's whimsical side. For discovery, enchantment, a snowfall, a child's dream.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo, metre | 3/4 at 120 to 160 (a waltz lilt), or 6/8; rubato feel through expression |
| Key, mode | Lydian (the raised 4th), major with chromatic mediants, occasional whole-tone colours |
| Harmony | `I II I`, `I bVI I`, `I III vi`, major 7ths and add9s; chords move by thirds |
| Melody | a twinkling motif with a chromatic turn or a raised 4th, arpeggio fragments, a long soaring answer in the strings |
| Rhythm | light: harp arpeggios, pizzicato-like shorts, a waltz pulse |
| Orchestra | harp and flute as the sparkle, high violins tremolo, violins and celli for the soaring answer, horns warm, choir "a" high and soft |
| Form | sparkle intro (harp, flute), the motif, the soaring answer, a mysterious turn (third relation), the motif returns tiny at the end |
| Signature devices | celesta-like high harp notes, string tremolo shimmer, chord shifts by a third, an ending on a major 7th or add9 chord |
| Pitfalls | quoting Hedwig's theme or other famous fantasy motifs; heavy low brass; dense percussion |
| In this studio | `harp`, `flute`, `violins` tremolo and long, `violas` long, `celli` long, `horns` long softly, `choir` sopranos and altos |
