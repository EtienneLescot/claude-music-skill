# Dark, suspense

Sounds like: Hans Zimmer's thrillers, Jóhann Jóhannsson, Marco Beltrami, Bernard Herrmann. For tension, investigation, a villain, horror.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo, metre | a pulse at 90 to 130, or no pulse at all; 4/4, sometimes 5/4 or 7/8 for unease |
| Key, mode | minor, Phrygian (the flat 2nd), Locrian; tritones and half steps |
| Harmony | static: one chord or a pedal for a long time; `i bII i`, `i bvi i`; clusters voiced softly |
| Melody | very little: a two- or three-note cell (a half step, a tritone) repeated, displaced, transposed |
| Rhythm | a low ostinato in 8ths or 16ths (celli and basses), irregular hits, sudden silences |
| Orchestra | low strings and low brass, tremolo violins high, timpani and big drum hits, anvil or metal accents, a choir on a low hum or a cluster |
| Form | slow build of the ostinato and the register, a stinger (a sudden loud hit), a drop to almost nothing, a final unresolved chord |
| Signature devices | a pedal under moving dissonances, a rising ostinato pitch by half steps, a sudden silence before the hit |
| Pitfalls | a pretty melody; resolving cadences; too much reverb hiding the rhythm; constant loudness |
| In this studio | `celli`, `basses` short for the ostinato; `violins` tremolo; `bass trombones`, `tuba` long for low swells; `timpani`, `big drum`, `anvil`; `choir` tenors and basses on "o" |
